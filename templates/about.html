Our Story

PKS & Associates has been a trusted name in the field of chartered accountancy for over 15 years. Founded with a vision to provide comprehensive financial and business solutions, we have grown to become one of the leading CA firms in the region.

Our journey began with a simple mission: to help businesses navigate the complex world of finance, taxation, and compliance with confidence and ease. Today, we serve over 500 clients across various industries, from startups to large corporations.

We pride ourselves on our commitment to excellence, integrity, and personalized service. Our team of qualified professionals brings together extensive experience and deep expertise to deliver solutions that are tailored to each client's unique needs.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional chartered accountancy services that empower businesses to achieve their financial goals while maintaining the highest standards of compliance and ethics.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and respected chartered accountancy firm, known for our expertise, integrity, and commitment to client success in the digital age.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Client-centricity, Innovation, and Continuous Learning form the foundation of everything we do at PKS & Associates.
